Expert answers to help you make informed decisions about LED displays

Professional-grade LED displays typically last 1 lakh working hours of continuous operation, translating to 10-15 years of normal use. Our displays come with Vivid Prime warranty and are built with premium components to ensure long-term reliability and consistent performance.

Selection depends on several factors: viewing distance (pixel pitch), indoor vs outdoor installation (brightness requirements), content type, budget, and specific application needs. Our expert team conducts free site surveys and provides customized recommendations based on your unique requirements.

LED displays require minimal maintenance - typically quarterly cleaning and annual professional inspections. Our displays feature hot-swappable modules for easy repairs without system downtime. We offer comprehensive maintenance packages with preventive care, remote monitoring, and 24/7 technical support.

Yes, our outdoor LED displays are engineered with IP65+ weatherproof ratings, operating reliably in temperatures from -20°C to 50°C. They feature advanced thermal management, sealed cabinets, and corrosion-resistant materials to withstand rain, dust, humidity, and direct sunlight.

Our displays support all major video formats (HDMI, DisplayPort, SDI), resolutions up to 8K, and integrate with leading control systems. We provide user-friendly software for content management, scheduling, and remote control. Cloud-based solutions and mobile apps are also available for convenient management.

Modern LED displays are highly energy-efficient, consuming 40-60% less power than traditional displays. Our systems feature intelligent brightness adjustment, power-saving modes, and optional solar integration. Most installations see ROI within 2-3 years through reduced energy costs and increased engagement.
